Our story

Started at a home office. Now operating in 32 states.

WinCap started in 2021. Our founder, Michael Collins, kept running into the same thing across the industry: smaller advisors and their clients getting treated like an afterthought. He wanted to build a firm where that never happened.

It began as a one-person shop run out of Michael's home office. Today we're headquartered in downtown Boston and work with clients in 32 states, but the way we do things hasn't changed. Everyone gets a real advisor and real attention, whatever the size of their portfolio.

That's the point of staying boutique: your goals set the agenda, and you're never just an account number to us.

Philosophy

Every dollar should have a job.

Since everyone's situation is different, we don't hand out one-size-fits-all portfolios. We build yours around what you actually need, covering your day-to-day first, then going after growth.

The Lifestyle Runway
The years of spending you keep safe in bonds. That's the runway that lets your investments recover and climb.
Just as a plane needs runway to take off, your Lifestyle Runway buys your investments time to climb back after a downturn. That way you can stay the course instead of selling at the bottom.
The chart above is a simplified illustration of the concept. It is not a projection, a guarantee of any outcome, or a representation of the past or future performance of any WinCap portfolio.

  • Cover your life first

    Before anything else, we set aside enough safe, high-quality bonds to protect the years of spending you'd want covered if the market turns.

  • Then go after growth

    With that safety net in place, we pick individual stocks, funds, and ETFs. We keep an eye on taxes and costs, and work around whatever you already hold.

  • Think in years, not charts

    We'd rather measure risk as years of spending you've got covered than as some volatility number. How long that runway is really comes down to you.
